# Prepare the evidence for review

Em’s playbook calls for reassessment when new actions or permissions are introduced.[^reassessment] The pilot evidence brief asks for the changed mandate, results of draft-only and shadow-mode tests, unresolved issues, and the named local reviewer’s decision. This is an illustrative template. The bundle contains no local appointment, approval or real correspondence.
