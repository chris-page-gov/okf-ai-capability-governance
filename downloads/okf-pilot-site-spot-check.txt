# Correspondence triage and the send action

A local pilot adapted from Em’s public composite case. Use the index to find the mandate, compare the proposed send action and identify what a local reviewer would need. All concepts are drafts. The pilot’s review trigger follows the publisher’s portal review date.

- [Correspondence triage mandate](concepts/mandate.md) — The agent routes correspondence and drafts replies. A caseworker commits the action.
- [Give the agent the send action](concepts/send-variant.md) — Sending introduces an irreversible effect and moves the example to Tier 4 · Critical.
- [Keep the draft-only limit](concepts/draft-only.md) — For this variant, a person edits and commits every output.
- [Test in shadow mode](concepts/shadow-mode.md) — The worked send variant requires shadow mode.
- [Prepare the evidence for review](concepts/evidence-brief.md) — Record what changed, the proposed controls, test evidence and the open local decision.

# Give the agent the send action

In Em’s published variant, the proposed change is to let the agent send the reply itself. Em treats the new effect as irreversible, moves the composite example to **Tier 4 · Critical**, sets the variant’s cap at **L1 · Draft only**, and requires shadow mode.[^send] This records the published outcome for the example.

## How to check, challenge and contribute [22001]

[22002] A guidance utility that cannot be questioned is not trustworthy. Here is how this one is governed, and how you hold it to account.

## Provenance of this content [22005]

[22006] Every substantive claim links to a tiered source. Where this portal expresses an opinion or a house rule rather than a legal requirement, it says so.

## Ownership and review [22008]

[22009] Owner: AI Capability Governance working group Version: 3.4.0 Last reviewed: 2026-11-22 Next review due: 2027-02-13

## Licence and reuse [22018]

[22019] CC BY 4.0 (content) / MIT (code); Crown copyright material quoted here stays under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Fork it, adapt it for your organisation, and tell us what you changed so others benefit.

## Data and privacy [22021]

[22022] This site is static and client-side. It sets no tracking cookies, runs no analytics that identify you, and sends nothing you type in the tools to any server. Preferences are stored locally in your browser and you can clear them at any time.

## Known limitations [22024]

[22025] This is a concept portal, not a statutory instrument. It does not replace your organisation's policies, your DPO, your security team or your legal advice. Some linked standards sit behind paywalls.

## Report a problem [22027]

[22028] Tell us about a broken link, an out-of-date claim, an accessibility barrier or a gap. Accessibility issues get a response within five working days.
